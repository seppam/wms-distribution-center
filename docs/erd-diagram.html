🔄 WMS Business Flow — Table Sequence

📥 Inbound Flow

1
Create PO/ASN in ERP → Import to inbound_receipts
↓
2
Truck arrives at dock → Update receipt status to in_progress
↓
3
Receive goods → Add lines to receipt_items, record qty
↓
4
QC check → Mark damaged items in receipt_items
↓
5
Put-away → Assign locations, write to inventory
↓
6
Record stock movement in inventory_transactions

📤 Outbound Flow

1
Create order → Insert into outbound_orders + order_items
↓
2
Wave planning → Generate pick_tasks from order items
↓
3
Picker picks from location → Update pick_task status
↓
4
Pack items → Create pack_list records
↓
5
Ship → Create shipments, deduct inventory
↓
6
All movements logged in inventory_transactions
